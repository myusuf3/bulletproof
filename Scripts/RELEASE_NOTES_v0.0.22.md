## What's new in 0.0.22

- A smarter Bulletproof Proofreader. It now fixes grammar the old model
  left alone: wrong prepositions ("up to another pr" -> "up in another
  pr"), verb patterns ("suggested me to rename" -> "suggested I rename")
  and word order. It still keeps how you write: slang, lowercase, "y'all",
  "ain't" and regional English stay as they are. In our tests it passes
  93% of cases, up from 86%.
- Already downloaded the Bulletproof Proofreader? Open Settings > Models
  and click the update button next to it (2.2 GB). Your current model
  keeps working until the update finishes.
- Switching engines no longer unloads a downloaded model that dictation
  is still using.
