## What's new in 0.0.12

- Models now show speed and accuracy ratings at a glance: a blue
  speedometer bar for speed and an orange target bar for accuracy.
- The Models table sits cleaner on the window with a Cloud/Offline
  column - installed models show their size, cloud models a one-click
  download.
- Fixed the Settings window opening at a squished size with the
  minimize and zoom buttons disabled; it now opens as a normal,
  centered window.
- The welcome and How to Use screens lost their title bar for a
  cleaner, edge-to-edge look.
