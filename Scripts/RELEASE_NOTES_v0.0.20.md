## What's new in 0.0.20

- Proofreading now fixes your mistakes and leaves everything else alone.
  Casual writing stays casual: lowercase stays lowercase, and slang,
  abbreviations, contractions, emoji, stretched words ("sooo") and
  deliberate CAPS are kept. Formal writing and dictation still get proper
  capitals and punctuation.
- Your own formats are kept as you typed them: times (6pm), units (5kg),
  British spellings (colour), links, @mentions, file paths, `code`, line
  breaks and bullet lists.
- Dictation gets its own proofreading pass, tuned for speech: it adds
  punctuation and capitals and fixes misheard words without rewording what
  you said.
- Safer pastes: if the model answers your text instead of correcting it,
  drops part of it, or adds things you didn't write, bulletproof shows an
  error and leaves your text untouched.
- Grammar fixes that remove extra words ("despite of the rain" ->
  "despite the rain") are no longer blocked.
- Apple Intelligence corrections are now consistent from run to run.
