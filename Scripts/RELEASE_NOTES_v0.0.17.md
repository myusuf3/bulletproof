## What's new in 0.0.17

- New Practice pane: bulletproof now notices which words it fixes for you
  again and again and turns them into a once-a-day, Monkeytype-style typing
  drill - with a streak to keep. It unlocks after five recurring typos.
  Same privacy line as everything else: single words only, stored on this
  Mac, never sent anywhere.
- A subtle chime when a correction lands in place, so you don't have to
  catch the flash. On by default; toggle it in Settings > General.
- The first-launch walkthrough got a redesign: dark glass, a proper hero
  welcome, gentle motion, and a small sound palette. First-run only -
  existing installs won't see it unless you replay it from Settings.
