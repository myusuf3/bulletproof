## What's new in 0.0.18

- Dictation: hold ⌥Space in any app and speak - let go, and what you said
  is typed at the cursor. A small pill at the bottom of the screen shows a
  live voice wave while bulletproof listens. It only listens while you hold
  the keys, and transcribes on this Mac.
- Pick your speech-to-text model in Settings > Dictation: Apple Speech
  (built into macOS, nothing to download) or Cactus Whistle, a 17 MB
  offline model that handles English, German, French, Spanish, Italian,
  Dutch, and Polish. Whistle downloads the moment you choose it.
- Choose which microphone dictation uses, and test it with a live meter.
  Optionally proofread dictated text before it's typed.
- bulletproof now asks for microphone access - in the first-launch
  walkthrough for new installs, and the first time you dictate for
  everyone else. You can also grant it in Settings > Dictation.
