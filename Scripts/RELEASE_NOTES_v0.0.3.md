## What's new in 0.0.3

- **Menu bar activity indicator.** The seal pulses while a proofread is in
  flight (only when it takes long enough to wonder about) and blips orange
  briefly when one fails. Respects Reduce Motion.
- **Launch at login.** New toggle in Settings > Shortcut.
- **Shortcuts support.** A "Proofread Text" action is available in the
  Shortcuts app and via Siri ("Proofread text with bulletproof") - text in,
  corrected text out, on-device as always.

### Requirements

- macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later, Apple silicon
- Apple Intelligence enabled for on-device proofreading
