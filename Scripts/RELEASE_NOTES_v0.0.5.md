## What's new in 0.0.5

Settings got a proper redesign:

- Tabs are now General, Engine, and Models - shortcut, launch at login,
  permissions, and the walkthrough live in General.
- Each pane sizes to its content (no more empty window below the controls).
- The engine pane shows a plain summary until a local model is installed,
  instead of a one-option radio button.
- Status text colors now meet contrast guidelines.
