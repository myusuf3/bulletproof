## What's new in 0.0.21

- A new proofreading model trained for bulletproof: a fine-tuned Qwen3 4B
  that runs on your Mac. In our tests it passes 96% of cases, up from 91%
  for the previous local model, and it never answered text that only
  looked like a request. It's listed first in the model picker and is a
  2.2 GB download.
- Once the new model finishes downloading, bulletproof switches to it
  automatically. If you've ever picked an engine yourself, your choice is
  kept.
- New app icon.
- New menu bar icons: one at rest, an animated one while proofreading,
  and a dictation icon while hold-to-talk is listening or transcribing.
