## What's new in 0.0.19

- The microphone now turns off as soon as you're done. The mic test in
  Settings > Dictation (and in the first-launch walkthrough) used to listen
  the whole time the pane was open - it's now a Test button that listens
  for a few seconds, then releases the mic.
- Dictation can never leave the mic on: if the shortcut's release is ever
  missed, recording stops on its own after five minutes and what you said
  is still typed.
